## Thumbnail concepts (≤3 words, one subject, no supernatural imagery, text never repeats the title)

**1. "800 TONNES" (recommended for the working title)**
- Subject: one Trilithon block, shot low and tight, with a single tiny human silhouette standing at its base for scale.
- Contrast: warm honey-coloured limestone against a deep blue dusk sky. White text with a thin black stroke, top-left.
- Why it works: a number plus visible scale is readable at feed size and true to the video.

**2. "THEY WALKED?"**
- Subject: one moai, slightly tilted, with three ropes running out of frame (real photo plus a subtle rope composite, or a 3D render clearly in reconstruction style).
- Contrast: dark grey-brown stone against a bright overcast sky. Yellow text.
- Accuracy note: the question mark matters. The walking hypothesis is strongly supported, not proven for every statue.

**3. "85 M DOWN"**
- Subject: a vertical cutaway of Derinkuyu's levels with one lamp-lit figure at the bottom.
- Contrast: black rock with a single warm lamp glow and a cyan depth line down the left edge.
- Accuracy note: ~85 m is the estimated depth of the full complex according to Turkey's Ministry of Culture and Tourism, and the script states it. Don't add "20,000 people".
